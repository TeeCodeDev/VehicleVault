## ✨ What's New

### 🏷️ 1. Sold & Archived Vehicle Lifecycle
- **Mark Vehicles as Sold (`SellVehicleModal`):** Record `sold_date` and `sold_price` while preserving all fuel, maintenance, and document history.
- **Automatic Showcase Take-Down:** Marking a vehicle as `sold` or `archived` automatically takes its public sales page offline (`is_active = 0`) unless explicitly kept open when selling (`keep_showcase_open`).
- **Clean Active Workspace:** Sold and archived vehicles are excluded from active fleet TCO totals, fixed-expense projections past `sold_date`, header vehicle selection, notification settings lists, and public showcase access.

### 📊 2. Fleet TCO Categories & Period Comparison
- **Main Vehicle Categories:** Filter Fleet TCO by `Auto's`, `Motoren`, `Bedrijfswagens`, or `Aanhangers` (`GET /api/analytics/fleet?vehicleType=...`).
- **Year-over-Year & Monthly Trend (`PeriodComparisonCard`):** Grouped monthly bar chart and delta summary comparing two selected years (or the latest month vs. previous month) for both individual vehicles and the entire fleet.
- **Compare Previous Vehicles:** Optional `?includeInactive=true` toggle in Fleet Analytics to benchmark active vehicles against previously sold or archived vehicles without polluting active garage totals.

### 💾 3. Scheduled Server Backups & Restore Points
- **Automated Backup Scheduler (`ToadScheduler`):** Configure automatic ZIP backups (SQLite database + uploads) every 12h, 24h, or 7 days with automatic retention cleanup in `DATA_DIR/backups`.
- **1-Click Restore Points UI (`AutoBackupCard`):** Create server backups on demand and restore, download, or delete stored restore points directly from Settings → Backup.

### 🇳🇱 4. RDW Recall Tracker, Extended Specs & Error Handling
- **Open Recall Alerts & Badge:** Detects `openstaande_terugroepactie_indicator` from RDW data, shows an `Open recall` badge on `VehicleCard`, and dispatches urgent notifications with a 30-day cooldown.
- **Extended RDW Specifications:** Collapsible RDW specs section on `VehicleCard` displaying color, curb/running weight, braked/unbraked towing capacity, catalog price, and European vehicle category.
- **Hardened RDW Lookup:** URL-encodes license plate queries and surfaces network/HTTP/timeout errors clearly with an 8-second request timeout.

### 🌍 5. Modular i18n Refactoring (by [@B0bbyD0llar](https://github.com/B0bbyD0llar))
- Merged PRs **#21, #22, #23, #24, #25, #26, #27, and #28**, moving hardcoded inline strings across Home Assistant, Notifications, 2FA/Passkey/OIDC, Profile, Users, Mail/Web Push, Dashboard/Navbar/Layout, and `inspectionTerms.ts` into modular `nl`, `en`, and `de` translation files.
